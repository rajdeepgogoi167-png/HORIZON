In an emergency, the hard part isn’t the blood. It’s finding it in time.

Blood Link puts verified donors, hospitals and doctors nearby in one place, so families spend minutes on the search instead of hours calling around.

Sign up once. Then everything is a tap away.

  • Pick your role

    Register as a patient, doctor or hospital. Add your blood group if you want to donate.

  • Share your location

    Doctors and hospitals near you show first. Location is shared only when you allow it.

  • Verified profiles

    Doctors are verified before they appear in search, so you only see confirmed profiles.

  • Emergency first

    If what you describe sounds urgent, Blood Link shows emergency guidance before anything else.

Care that’s close to you, and matched to what you’re going through.

Find verified doctors, get hospital suggestions for your symptoms, and request blood when it matters.

Doctors in your area

Compare verified doctors by specialty, city, fee and rating, then book a clinic visit or a video consult.

Hospitals for your condition

Describe how you feel, by text or voice, and get hospital and specialty suggestions. Blood Link suggests care. It doesn’t diagnose.

Blood requests, matched

Post a request for your blood group, or respond to one nearby. Compatible donors are matched by standard blood group rules.

Lab tests at home

Book a lab test and choose home sample collection, so you don’t have to travel to the lab.

Donate often. Earn trust. Get rewarded.

Every verified donation adds to a donor’s trust level, so people who need blood can see who has reliably come through before.

  1. Register as a donor

    Add your blood group and contact details. Your profile starts at a new-donor level.

  2. Donate and get verified

    Each donation is confirmed before it counts toward your record.

  3. Climb the trust levels

    Regular, verified donation moves you up. Requesters see your level on your profile.

  4. Unlock milestone rewards

    Reach a donation milestone and receive a reward. The rewards are set by the program.
